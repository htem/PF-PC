 [G_con](./G_con.graphml) and [G_touch](./G_touch.graphml) are graph representations of GrC/PF-PC synaptic connectivity/touches. 
 They are extracted from [htem/cb2_project_analysis/tree/main/graphs](https://github.com/htem/cb2_project_analysis/tree/main/graphs).  

 [G_AB_con](./G_AB_con.graphml) and [G_AB_touch](./G_AB_touch.graphml) are mappings of the differential connectivity/touches of the ascending branches and parallel fibers of local GrCs. They can be used, e.g., to calculate correlations between the connectivity of ABs and PFs.
